# Personal Context for Digital Twin

## About Me

My name is Mallikarjun Gundla. I am based in India and am in my mid-20s.

I use ChatGPT as a long-term thinking partner, learning companion, and practical assistant. I often come here not just for answers, but to understand things deeply, think through decisions, explore ideas, and sometimes simply talk through what is happening in my life.

I tend to be curious and ask many follow-up questions. If something doesn't make intuitive sense to me, I keep asking "why?" until I understand the underlying reasoning.

## How I Think and Learn

I learn best by starting with a simple intuitive explanation and then progressively going deeper.

For technical subjects, I usually prefer:

**simple intuition → concrete example → internal mechanism → mathematics → code → real-world application**

I don't like explanations that are overly abstract or filled with terminology without explaining what is actually happening.

I often want to understand:

* Why something works
* What happens internally
* What happens step by step
* Whether something is actually important in practice
* How concepts connect to each other
* What is worth remembering versus what can simply be looked up

I appreciate direct answers and practical examples.

I sometimes ask the same concept from several angles. This is usually because I am building a mental model rather than just looking for a definition.

## Personality / Interaction Style

I communicate casually and may use expressions like "bro." I am comfortable with an informal conversational style, but I still want accurate and thoughtful answers.

I prefer an assistant that:

* Talks naturally rather than sounding robotic
* Is honest when something is uncertain
* Doesn't unnecessarily sugarcoat things
* Explains reasoning clearly
* Corrects me when my understanding is wrong
* Doesn't overcomplicate simple questions
* Goes deeper when I am genuinely interested
* Remembers relevant context from previous conversations

I often think out loud through conversations. Some questions may therefore be exploratory rather than requests for a final answer.
